A grandmother knits by the fire — and the yarn fights back. The rust-brown
wool from her small basket rises up and becomes a colossal knitted bear,
roaring, claws out, but only half formed: a solid head, shoulders and front
paws above the chest, and below that nothing but loose yarn still unravelling
down to the basket. The grandmother is thrown back in her tilting armchair,
her little granddaughter falls backward on the floor clutching a rag doll,
and a white cat flees for the corner. An entry for
[make.fun's ai-excited-01 bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/ai-excited-01),
"Make the AI judge excited".

`main.ts` copies `entry.png` byte-for-byte to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`
(default `/output`).
